Chat usage

Reading your open chats...

The percentages are Anthropic's own, the numbers /usage shows. Anthropic does not split them per chat, so each chat's share is an estimate: how far the meter moved divided by how much every chat used over the same time, weighted by model price. Usage on claude.ai or the phone app is not in these transcripts, so it is spread over the chats here. Dollar figures are API-rate equivalents; nothing is billed. Context is what every step re-reads. Next step is the price of the chat's next step: cheap while the prompt cache is warm, about 12 to 20 times more once it has expired (after 5 minutes or an hour, depending on the chat). A model can only be switched inside the chat: type /model sonnet or /model opus there. Close + handoff asks a throwaway copy of the chat for a handoff note (one step, about the wake price), saves it to ~/.claude/handoffs, then stops the chat. A VS Code tab stays open but stops answering; a terminal chat exits.
